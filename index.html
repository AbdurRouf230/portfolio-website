About me

Curious builder, from circuits to the cloud

My programming journey

I started Computer Science and Engineering at Independent University, Bangladesh in 2022. My first real program was a Java OOP project (a movie production house manager), and soon after I was writing algorithms for competitions and wiring logic gates for a line-following robot. Those early wins at IUB Techfest (Champion in the algorithm track, runner-up with the LFR bot) got me hooked on building things people can see and use.

From there I went two ways at once. On the hardware side I learned Arduino and ESP32, which grew into research on running neural networks on microcontrollers. On the software side I joined Programming Hero's MERN Stack course, moving from plain HTML and CSS pages to JavaScript, React, Firebase, Node, Express, MongoDB and Next.js.

Work I enjoy

I love turning a design into a clean, responsive interface and then wiring it to real data. My favourite projects sit where the web meets machine learning, like Flood Shield, where a React dashboard talks to an Express API and a Python model. I enjoy problems with a clear social impact: emergency services, flood response, air quality around schools.

Beyond the keyboard

Outside programming I tinker with electronics: RC cars, sensor kits and a CanSat with RF modules and pressure sensors. I enjoy competing in tech fests with friends, I'm learning French, and I like reading research papers on remote sensing more than I probably should.

Research & hands-on work

  1. Research · Embedded ML

    Thermal Sensing on Microcontrollers

    Collected datasets with AMG8833 (8×8) and MLX90640 (32×24) thermal sensors, trained a dual-head CNN for occupancy / posture classification and heat-source localisation, quantised it to INT8 and deployed it on an ESP32.

    Corresponding author: Scientific Reports (under review). Co-author: SOCTA 2026.

  2. Research · Remote Sensing & Geospatial ML

    PM2.5 Exposure & Brick-Kiln Vulnerability Mapping

    National-scale machine-learning framework that combines satellite trace-gas retrievals, aerosol data and brick-kiln proximity to estimate PM2.5 exposure for 18k-34k geolocated schools across Bangladesh.

    Elsevier RSASE (under review) and two papers submitted to IEEE/ACM BDCAT 2026.
